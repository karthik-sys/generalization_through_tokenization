# Full-project diagnostic audit — 2026-08-20

Everything measured to date, ranked honestly, compared against SOTA, with next-day recipe proposals at the end. All numbers now also live in `results/metrics.json` (routed24–28 and routed31–33 were backfilled today; they had only ever been reported in chat).

## 0. Data hygiene — read this before the tables

1. **Two LAMBADA populations.** Arms evaluated at full n=5153 (routed15–18, 20, 21, 24, 25, 26, routed8's corrected read) are directly comparable to each other. Arms evaluated at n=500 (routed28, 31, 32, 33, and everything before the n=5153 correction) ride the easy-biased head of the unshuffled stream: **optimistic by ~0.5pp EM, noise ±1.7pp**. Cross-population EM gaps under ~2pp are not real until re-scored.
2. **BPB is internal.** Single/cross-domain BPB is measured on our own held-out domain streams, not a public benchmark — valid for arm-vs-arm ranking, not for external comparison.
3. **Nothing has ever been seed-replicated.** Every "winner" is n=1. Decisions keyed on <1pp EM are keyed on noise.
4. **Two arms have missing final BPB** (routed31@94k, routed32@222k) due to an unresolved Modal-side eval cancellation specific to the ModernBackbone path (13 failed attempts total; see `overnight_eval_summary.md`).

## 3. SOTA comparison

**Internal baselines (same data, same evals):**

| Arm | single BPB | LAMBADA EM | LAMBADA ppl |
|---|---|---|---|
| baseline (unified 48k BPE, 69M) | 2.0049 | 0.0% | 7,786 |
| sota arm (cl100k vocab, 122M) | 2.1071 | 0.0% | 66,681 |
| baseline-large (unified BPE, 160M) | **1.3543** | 0.8% | 832 |
| best routed (routed33/25) | 1.72–1.83 | 16.6–18.0% | 6.5–7.9 |

The core thesis result stands at every scale: unified-vocab baselines compress better in-domain (**1.354 is still the project's best single BPB**) but are catastrophically incapable of LAMBADA-style retrieval (0–0.8% vs 16–18%). routed19's phase-1 read (1.3554 single BPB on the routed architecture, before switching training) proves the *architecture* can match baseline compression — the gap is a training-recipe artifact (diet erosion), not an architectural tax.

**External anchor — GPT-2 (Radford et al. 2019), the only shared benchmark:**

| Model | Params | LAMBADA acc |
|---|---|---|
| GPT-2 small | 117M | 45.99% |
| GPT-2 medium | 345M | 55.48% |
| **ours (best, stopword-filtered)** | 90–222M | **20.8% (n=5153) / 23.6–23.8% (n=500)** |

We're at roughly **half of GPT-2-small** on its home benchmark, at comparable param count but a fraction of its data/compute (≤8.5B tokens vs WebText-scale training). GPT-2's reported LAMBADA *perplexity* (35.13 for small) is word-level and **not comparable** to our target-token ppl (~6.5–8) — do not quote those side by side. The trajectory across our own generations is the meaningful signal: 0.4% → 6.2% → 9.9% → 14.8% → 16.6% → ~18% EM over the project's lifetime, with no scaling beyond 222M.

## 4. Lever ledger (what's proven, what's dead)

**Confirmed levers** (each isolated by at least one controlled pair):
- **Copy-gate** (unbiased init): +3–4pp EM alone; works from scratch (routed22) and warm-started (routed11); holds at 190M (routed14).
- **Diet (70% nlp)**: +3pp EM (routed17 vs routed15); 83% adds nothing (routed26).
- **Gate × diet stacking**: super-additive — 14.75% (routed21) vs 9.9/8.9 individually.
- **Longer training of the stack**: 14.75% → 16.57% (routed25).
- **Scale 89M→190M**: same quality in <half the steps; best cross BPB (routed28).
- **Modern backbone**: C's 17.6% at 31% budget — strongest per-step trajectory anywhere.
- **Generalist 5th domain**: 18.0% from scratch without modern backbone or warm start (routed33).
- **Books as nlp source**: gentler math/science erosion, best 89M cross BPB (routed27).

**Dead / rejected:**
- GradNorm (routed2/3/pooled2), alignment loss (routed23 catastrophic alone, routed20≈routed21 neutral stacked), decoupled switch head (routed4, switch acc 0.000), curriculum staging (routed19 — conclusive monotonic phase-2 erosion), copy-structure mining (routed18 ≈ routed17), diet >70% (routed26), cl100k vocab (sota arm).

**Known open wounds:**
- **Diet erosion**: single BPB degrades monotonically in every long diet run (routed28: 1.58→1.80; math ppl 298→919). Nobody has tried annealing the diet.
- **Gradient conflict is measured, not fixed**: switch-loss vs LM-loss backbone gradients, cos = **−0.117, negative in 96.7% of batches** (routed25). Gradient surgery untried.
- The full stack (gate + diet + modern backbone + generalist) **has never been combined** — every winner above holds only a subset.

## 5. Proposed recipes — next day (3–5)

1. **routed34 — "the stack" (flagship bet).** C/D's tied-emb + modern backbone (D's RoPE+RMSNorm core; add SwiGLU/QK-norm since C ≥ D so far) + copy-gate + diet, 89M, from scratch, 300k. First-ever combination of the three strongest independent levers. Target: EM ≥ 19–20% full-set.
2. **routed35 — generalist merge (the planned routed33 follow-up).** Warm-start **routed28@140000** (its best-balance checkpoint, not the eroded 300k) + add the generalist 5th domain via the existing `_warm_start_routed33_generalist` path. Tests whether routed33's recipe transfers onto a mature backbone — the exact question routed33 was built to de-risk.
3. **routed36 — diet anneal (erosion fix).** routed25's recipe, but nlp share decays 70%→~30% over the final third with a hard ≥10% floor for math/science. Success criterion: hold EM ≥16% while single BPB ≤1.65 (routed19 phase-1 proves ≤1.36 is architecturally reachable). Directly attacks the only systematic quality regression in the flagship lineage.
4. **routed37 — gradient surgery (cheap, orthogonal).** PCGrad-style projection: drop the component of the switch-loss gradient that conflicts with the LM gradient on shared backbone params. Motivated by the measured −0.117 cosine. Run on the routed21 recipe for a clean A/B at 100k steps.
5. **Finish + measure (not a new arm, highest information per dollar).** Resume **C (routed31)** and **routed33** to 300k — the two best trajectories in the project are both unfinished. Re-score every n=500 arm at full n=5153 before final rankings. Route C/D's blocked BPB evals through the training pods' own eval path instead of Modal to sidestep the cancellation bug.

If GPU budget forces a choice: **#5 first** (finishing C and routed33 is cheaper than any new run and disambiguates the leaderboard), **#1 second** (the stack is the only recipe with a credible shot at ~20% EM without scaling).
